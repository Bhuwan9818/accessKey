Feature · Accessibility Widget

One widget, every reading & navigation aid

A single on-page panel that gives every visitor real-time control over contrast, text, navigation, and motion — no rebuild, no code changes.

Add to Your Site See How It Helps
What's Inside

Controls for how people read & browse

Every control is grouped clearly so visitors find what they need in seconds.

🔠

Text & Readability

Adjustable font size, letter and line spacing, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and a reading guide that follows the cursor.

🎨

Contrast & Colour

High-contrast, dark, and grayscale modes, plus saturation control for colour-sensitive visitors.

⌨️

Keyboard & Navigation

Visible focus outlines, skip-to-content shortcuts, and a clean tab order across your whole site.

⏸️

Motion & Animation Pause

One click freezes autoplay videos, carousels, and GIFs for visitors sensitive to motion or flashing content.

🔍

Zoom & Cursor

Page-wide zoom and an enlarged, high-contrast cursor for visitors with low vision or motor difficulty.

🎧

Screen-Reader Friendly

Built with proper ARIA roles and labels so the widget itself never gets in the way of assistive tech.

Why It Matters

Built for the people who need it most

Each control set maps to a real, common barrier — not a generic checklist.

👁️

Low vision & colour blindness

Larger text, stronger contrast, and grayscale/saturation controls make content legible without a screen reader.

📖

Dyslexia & reading difficulty

Wider letter spacing, a dyslexia-friendly font, and the reading guide reduce how much effort it takes to track a line.

🖱️

Motor impairment

Bigger click targets, keyboard-only navigation, and an enlarged cursor remove the need for fine mouse control.

🧠

ADHD & cognitive load

Pausing motion and simplifying layout cuts down on visual noise so visitors can focus on the content that matters.
