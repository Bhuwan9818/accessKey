What You Get

Everything you need in one dashboard

📊

Compliance Score

A real-time score showing how your site tracks against WCAG 2.2 AA, updated automatically as issues are fixed.

📜

Audit Logs

A timestamped history of every scan, fix, and configuration change — useful for internal review or legal documentation.

📄

VPAT Export

Generate a Voluntary Product Accessibility Template in one click, ready to share with procurement or legal teams.

Profile Usage Analytics
